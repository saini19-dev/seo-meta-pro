Privacy Policy

✅ No Data Collected

Last updated: May 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the SEO Meta Tags Analyzer - SEO Checker Pro Chrome Extension handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The extension does not gather, store, transmit, or share any user data whatsoever.

2. How The Extension Works

The extension reads SEO-related data from the currently active webpage in your browser. This includes:

  • Meta tags (title, description, keywords)
  • Heading structure (H1–H6)
  • Image attributes (ALT, title)
  • Link data (internal/external)
  • Open Graph and Twitter Card tags
  • Schema / Structured data
  • Tracking tags (Google Analytics, GTM, etc.)
  • Technical SEO attributes

All analysis is performed locally in your browser. No data is sent to any external server.

3. Data Storage

The extension does not store any data locally or remotely. Each analysis is performed fresh every time you open the extension popup.

4. Third Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any information to third parties. There are no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services used in this extension.

5. Permissions Used

  • activeTab — To read SEO data from the current tab only
  • scripting — To inject a local content script for data extraction
  • host permissions — To enable analysis on any website you visit

These permissions are used solely for SEO analysis and nothing else.

6. Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
